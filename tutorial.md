## How To Log A Discovery Point Using Anseo Digital Passport Digital Passport

1. Go to Anseodigitalpassport.com
1. If you are on a phone, click the three bars in the top right. Then click the Log In button
1. Sign up using either your Google Account or your email and password.
1. Once logged in, you can see the closest Discoverry Point and how far away it is
1. If you are within 25m of a point, you can log it by pressing the red button
1. After loggin you will be taken to the log viewer. From here you can upload an image and write a comment.
1. Thats It! You have just logged your first Discovery Point
