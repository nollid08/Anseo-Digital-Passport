About

Anseo Digital Passport is a digital logging service for the Wild Atlantic Way. It uses the GPS on your phone to see if you are anywhere near 1 of the 162 Discovery Points. If you are within 30m of one, Anseo will allow you to “log” it. When you log it, you can take a picture and write a comment. All your logs are are saved to your digital “Passport” for you to view anytime, anywhere.
